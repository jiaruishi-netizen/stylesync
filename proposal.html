StyleSync is an AI stylist for the closet you've already got — not the one an app wants to sell you.

StyleSync turns your own wardrobe into a real outfit — for the weather, the occasion, the dress code, today. Upload what you own, tell it your taste, and get 2–3 outfits explained in plain language, built only from clothes you already have.

Built by Jiarui Shi Format Individual AI web app Primary user Anyone who owns clothes they like but freezes at the closet

The closet isn't the problem.
The decision is.

01 / Problem

Most people already own clothes they like — they just can't turn a full closet into "the presentation is at 2pm, it's 60 degrees, and I want to look put-together but not stiff." Fashion apps are built to sell the next thing, not to help with the thing already hanging in the closet.

StyleSync starts from what you own. Optional purchases only show up once your existing wardrobe has been used — clearly labeled, never mixed in as if you already had them.

What it won't compromise on

02 / Values
Own It

Wear what you own

Recommendations start with your closet, not a cart. Purchases are optional, separate, and always labeled as such.

Trust

Your data, your call

Every AI tag is editable. Every photo is deletable. You choose whether purchase suggestions appear at all.

Fit

Grounded in you, explained plainly

Every outfit is built only from items you actually own, with a short reason attached — never an invented item, never a mystery.
