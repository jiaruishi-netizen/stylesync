What should I pack?

Packing is the opposite problem from getting dressed: you're choosing once, for days you can't see yet, with a bag that only holds so much. StyleSync picks the smallest set of clothes you own that still covers the trip — favouring pieces that go with everything, pack flat, and don't arrive creased.
